TRANSPARENT PRICING

Rate and terms

10% per month, flat rate. Long-standing members may qualify for a reduced rate of 8%. Flat rate means interest is calculated on your original loan amount rather than a reducing balance, so your monthly repayment stays the same for the life of the loan. Facilities are short-term.

What we accept as collateral

Vehicles

Secured by a signed Vehicle Sales Agreement. On default, ownership of the pledged vehicle transfers to the cooperative.

Business assets

Stock, equipment, and other verifiable business assets.

A guarantor

Required alongside collateral. Your guarantor signs a Letter of Guarantee and provides an undated signed cheque covering the facility.

What you'll need to apply

Home address, and business address if applying as a business.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to be a member to borrow?

Yes. Loans are issued through Fancrest Thrift and Credit Cooperative Society Ltd to its members. Membership is ₦5,000 per year, non-refundable.

What can I use as collateral?

Vehicles, secured by a Vehicle Sales Agreement, and verifiable business assets. A guarantor is required alongside your collateral.

What documents do I need to apply?

A completed membership form, valid government ID, your BVN, a passport photograph, address details, and a guarantor who provides a Letter of Guarantee and an undated signed cheque.

What happens if I miss a payment?

For vehicle-secured loans, ownership of the pledged vehicle transfers to the cooperative on default, as set out in your signed agreement. Your guarantor is also liable for the outstanding balance.

How long does approval take?

As soon as everything, including your collateral, has been checked and approved.

Can I repay early, and does that reduce the interest?

No. Interest is calculated on your original loan amount for the agreed term, so repaying early does not reduce the total interest due.
