About Fancrest

Incorporated with the Corporate Affairs Commission and operating from a physical office in Garki.

Who we are

Fancrest Investment Limited is a financial and contracting firm based in Abuja, Nigeria, registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission under RC 8961606.

We provide collateral-backed lending, financial advisory, investment, and contracting services to individuals and businesses combining sound financial practice with a client-first approach.

Our goal is simple: to help clients make confident financial decisions and achieve lasting growth.

Our mission

To provide accessible, transparent, collateral-backed funding that helps individuals and businesses across Nigeria grow.

Our vision

To be a trusted financial partner known for integrity, transparency, and dependable service across Nigeria.
